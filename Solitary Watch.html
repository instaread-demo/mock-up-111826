NewsMatch 2026: Every Time You Give, You’re Giving Twice.

by Jean Casella | November 16, 2026

Dear Friends,

Listen to audio version of this article
Sponsored by
0:00 / 2:43
Instaread

Once a year—as part of the NewsMatch program for independent newsrooms—we take the time to check in with you about the variety and strength of the work we’ve done, the impact we’re seeing, and our plans for the upcoming year.

We show you what happens when you give to Solitary Watch, and why we’re asking you to give once again.

Matching support from NewsMatch means the first $20,000 we raise will be matched—dollar for dollar.
Your support—and impact—will be doubled today with NewsMatch. ➤

We take these moments seriously. We believe your time, money, and attention are valuable, and if we’re going to ask anything of you, it had better be for a good reason.

So when someone says, “If you can raise a dollar, I’ll give you a dollar, too,” it’s our obligation to let you know:

Giving to Solitary Watch during NewsMatch is the best way to turn how you feel about solitary confinement into how you do something about solitary confinement.
Help us keep making a difference. ➤

By now, you know we’re a small team, and that a gift of any size goes to work right away. It gives our reporters time to follow a story that might otherwise go untold. It helps us maintain connections with people in solitary who have stories to tell. And this year—for the first time—your dedicated support helped us keep the promise of Lifelines to Solitary by providing more people living in isolation with someone to write to and hear back from.

We may not be big, but we are mighty, and our work—the effect we’ve had on the public awareness of solitary confinement—has led to lasting changes in policies, laws, and actual lives.

We’ve always done a lot with very little, going all the way back to 17 years ago, when we were just two people with a blog.

That’s why opportunities like NewsMatch—and all matching gifts—are so important to us.
Your donation—and our impact—are supercharged thanks to NewsMatch. ➤

If you’re a longtime donor, now is the time to renew your support. If you’ve been reading Solitary Watch and haven’t given before, now is the time to make your first gift.

And if things are tight—as they are for too many—please consider a smaller, monthly gift: NewsMatch will double your annual total!

Thank you for reading, for your faith in our mission, and for donating in any amount so that we can keep on doing this good, hard thing.

With gratitude,

Jean Casella
Director


P.S.  If you want to support anywhere above the $1000 level please reach out to me directly at jcasella@solitarywatch.org. Any sizable gift can be used to further increase this year’s match!

No one deserves to be forgotten. ➤

Jean Casella

Jean Casella is the director and co-founder of Solitary Watch. She has also published work in The Guardian, The Nation, and Mother Jones, and is co-editor of the book Hell Is a Very Small Place: Voices from Solitary Confinement. She has received a Soros Justice Media Fellowship and an Alicia Patterson Fellowship. She tweets @solitarywatch.

Help Expose the Hidden World of Solitary Confinement

Accurate information and authentic storytelling can serve as powerful antidotes to ignorance and injustice. We have helped generate public awareness, mainstream media attention, and informed policymaking on what was once an invisible domestic human rights crisis.

Only with your support can we continue this groundbreaking work, shining light into the darkest corners of the U.S. criminal punishment system.
