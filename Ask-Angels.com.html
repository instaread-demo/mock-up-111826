September 29th we have the festival day of Michaelmas, also called the Feast of Saint Michael and the Feast of the Archangels.

I first learned about Michaelmas through the teachings of Rudolf Steiner who emphasized that true spiritual festivals are not arbitrary dates set by the Church, but are rather cosmic realities: reflections of the rhythms in the Earth, Sun, and spiritual hierarchies.

We see this with Michaelmas!

And because these festivals are cosmic rhythms rather than fixed dates on a page, they meet us differently depending on where we stand on the Earth.

So if you are reading this from the southern hemisphere, your light is returning right now rather than withdrawing. Michaelmas still arrives on September 29th… and Archangel Michael is not bound to one season, or to one half of the world. The courage he brings serves the turning inward. And it serves the stepping forward just as fully.

And the invitation it brings is alive for us now.

The Spiritual Meaning of Michaelmas

At the Autumn Equinox (September 22nd), day and night align in equal balance. But from this point forward, we move into the dark half of the year.

And as we do…

Archangel Michael steps forward to guide us. 

Archangel Michael is often depicted as the radiant warrior angel standing victorious over the dragon.

In esoteric teachings, when Archangel Michael slays the dragon, it is not seen to be a victory over against any external beast, but rather the victory against inner forces of fear, doubt, and materialism that can overwhelm the human spirit.

The dragon represents the heaviness that pulls us away from spiritual truth, those voices that whisper discouragement, confusion, and hopelessness.

Archangel Michael is seen not only as a saintly protector, but also as a cosmic being leading humanity into a new age of spiritual consciousness.

His task is to help us develop what Steiner called “heart-thinking,” aligning wisdom with love so that truth becomes not just an idea, but a living force we embody in the world.
