Fourteen Years of Trauma Starts Somewhere Some prequels explain the past. Others make you wish you could change it. Xboxachievements.com First, I owe everyone an apology because this review is criminally late. In my defense, I needed to play through the campaign twice before I could properly gather my thoughts, process everything I had experienced, and figure out how to put those feelings into words. The first playthrough was spent somewhere between screaming at my television, appreciating the sheer spectacle of it all, and remembering why I fell in love with this franchise in the first place. The second was about slowing down, paying attention to the details, and appreciating what The Coalition had accomplished. I am enamored. Gears of War: E-Day reminded me why this franchise has occupied such a special place in my gaming heart for nearly two decades. There is something incredibly satisfying about returning to a world you know intimately and discovering that it still has the capacity to surprise you, terrify you, and emotionally ruin your entire afternoon. The Coalition could have easily leaned on nostalgia, thrown Marcus and Dom into another blood-soaked battlefield, and called it a day. Instead, they delivered a prequel that understands what made Gears of War special while giving us a more intimate look at the people who would eventually become legends.
