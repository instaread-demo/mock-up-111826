The Weekend Watchlist: Nov. 19 to Nov. 22

Olivia Clemmensen, Austin Couto, and Weston Perry • November 18, 2026
(Jashodhara Jindal / Heights Editor)
Listen to audio version of this article
Sponsored by
0:00 / 4:59
Instaread

Wondering what to do once you clock out of O’Neill this Friday? The Weekend Watchlist is your guide to the places to be, on and off campus, every weekend.

Read on to see what’s coming up.

Editors’ Picks 

Olivia Clemmensen

Boston Thanksgiving Market & Harbor Festival

Friday to Sunday, Nov. 20 to Nov. 22 from 10 a.m. to 7:30 p.m. | Christopher Columbus Waterfront Park, Boston

With Thanksgiving right around the corner, these are the last few weekends of autumnal events before snowflakes come falling down. This Thanksgiving market brings together local artisans, handmade crafts, food vendors, boutique shops, and live music—all by the Boston Harbor. This is a perfect spot to walk around with friends and get a start on some holiday shopping by supporting local small businesses.

Details/Times: Here

Austin Couto 

BC Theatre Dept. Presents: A Midsummer Night’s Dream: A New Musical

Wednesday, Nov. 18 through Friday, Nov. 20 at 7:30 p.m., Sunday, Nov. 22 at 2 p.m. | Robsham Theater Main Stage 

Spend your weekend embracing culture on BC’s own campus. A Midsummer Night’s Dream: A New Musical, the theatre department’s premier musical of the school year, brings a refreshing new take to the Shakespeare classic. Support your peers, and get cultured. 

Tickets: Here

Weston Perry

Faneuil Hall Marketplace’s Annual Holiday Tree Lighting

Saturday, Nov. 21 from 2 to 6 p.m.| Quincy Market, Boston

Kick off your holiday season with a festive celebration at one of Boston’s most cherished traditions. Filled with live performances, shopping, and food, it is guaranteed to be a night full of Christmas cheer. The 30-foot Norway spruce will be lit at 6 p.m. sharp by Make-A-Wish and Santa. This weekend, a new indoor holiday market is coming to Faneuil Hall, so stop by and enjoy the festive spirit.

CAB Presents: Community Service Series at Cradles to Crayons

Thursday, Nov. 19 at 1 p.m. | 281 Newtonville Ave., Newtonville, Mass.

Join CAB for two hours as they volunteer at Cradles to Crayons to help end clothing insecurity for children. 

Tickets: Here 

CAB Presents: Puttshack

Thursday, Nov. 19 at 7 p.m. | 58 Pier 4 Blvd., Boston, Mass.

This CAB event mixes sports with fun as you mini-golf with your closest friends for a heavily discounted price. 

Tickets: Here

CAB Presents: Trivia Night

Thursday, Nov. 19 at 7 p.m. | The Rat

The Common Tones’ Fall Cafe

Enjoy a night of a cappella and fun at The Common Tones’ big fall show.
