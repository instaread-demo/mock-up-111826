A Particularly Bizarre Chinese Warlord Era Pistol

This pistol came up in some discussion, so I figured I ought to do a full video on it. It’s a particularly weird one, with C96-style hammer and safety, a fake detachable magazine (it actually feeds from stripper clips) and a horrible rear sight paired with a perfectly normal front sight. Oh, and the frame is brazed together and someone set a pair of Japanese occupation coins in the grips. And it’s not a one-off; I found a second example of the same type when writing Pistols of the Warlords.
