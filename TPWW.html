Zack Sabre Jr. Says He’s Hoping To Defend CMLL World Heavyweight Title at NJPW Wrestle Kingdom 21

As noted before, this past Monday’s co-promoted Lucha Kingdom event for NJPW and CMLL at the Arena Mexico in Mexico City, Mexico featured Zack Sabre Jr. defeating Hechicero to become the new CMLL World Heavyweight Champion.

Following the match, Sabre Jr. gave some comments to the press backstage and stated that he wants to return to CMLL before the end of 2026. Sabre Jr. also stated that he is also currently hoping to defend his CMLL World Heavyweight title at NJPW’s Wrestle Kingdom 21 event on January 4, 2027.

“It was a lot of Japanese fans in the audience too so, it’s a unique experience. Most foreign wrestlers probably wish to wrestle at Arena México once; check a list. I’ve wrestled here six or seven times this year, and I very much would like to be back before the end of the year. I have to go back to Japan tomorrow. We have Tag League, but, I don’t know. Before the end of the year, before the Tokyo Dome, it would be nice to come and defend this championship. I don’t wanna keep it hidden away but, obviously, with January 4th, Wrestle Kingdom coming up, I’ll be looking to defend the championship there as well. So it’s gonna be great. I think a match with Blue Panther would cross generations. I’m eager to face as much of the new generation as possible. I want them to come and find me. Obviously, now I’m gonna be here more; I’m gonna be paying a lot closer attention. The last couple of trips here, I’ve had big matches so I’ve not really been able to pay attention to the events. On my way back to Japan, now that I’m champion, I’m gonna be catching up with the next couple of weeks, especially the Mexico City shows, and get very familiar with my possible contenders.”

RYZIN Comments on His Decision To Retire From His Wrestling Career In 2027

As noted before, former WWE and AEW and current indies talent RYZIN recently announced that he will be retiring from his in-ring wrestling career in 2027.

A recent episode of Fightful Overbooked’s In The Weeds podcast featured RYZIN as the guest. One of the topics discussed included RYZIN’s thoughts about the mostly positive reception to his retirement announcement.

“So, the announcement is a little bittersweet to me, it kind of came as a almost a culture shock to me because yesterday, I was overwhelmed by the amount of people that were commenting, for better or worse, primarily mostly for the better. They were saying a lot of things, it was almost like watching your own funeral in a lot of ways because as a performer, when you announce that you’re stepping away from the business, then I think you start to get your flowers. When you’re actually performing on a week to week basis, you don’t always see those in a lot of ways.”

RYZIN also gave his thoughts about him recovering from a lumbar injury and his reason for his decision to retire from his wrestling career in 2027.

“As far as the back injury, I’m feeling pretty good. I’ve been doing decompression therapy every other Wednesday. It was a prolonged nagging issue that I had dealt with for like a year, continuing to wrestle on, to the point where I couldn’t even bend over to pick over a sock, so I knew there was definitely something going on. I’m not into taking medications and things like that, I don’t drink, I don’t smoke. I’m not trying to be this straight edge guru, it’s just not the way I ever was. I was trying every wholistic approach you could as far as healing it up. It was a good time in my career and there was a lot of things happening and changes happening on the independents and stuff to where I said, you know what, let me take a step back and let me heal for the rest of the year and see how I feel going into the new year. That’s how it pretty much started and then shortly there after, looking at the landscape of professional wrestling and independent wrestling, both from a business aspect and a financial side, I drew the conclusion that the juice has gotta be worth the squeeze. Even though my back is on the mend and I will be good to go going into the new year, I was just like, maybe it’s time to hang them up. I’ll be 41 in February, I’m not blind to the fact that the industry may have passed me by as I had my opportunities and never fully became that full time television star that I envisioned.”

Jonathan Gresham Launching New Promotion In January 2027

Former TNA and ROH and current indies talent Jonathan Gresham recently announced on Twitter that he will be launching a new promotion called Round1 Pro Wrestling in January of 2027.

Gresham also announced that tickets for his new upcoming promotion’s debut event on January 9, 2027 in Atlanta, Georgia are currently on sale.
