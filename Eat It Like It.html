Four Great Florida Cuban Sandwiches

Being very honest, I don’t stop for them often. With one exception I will share shortly. The Cuban sandwich is Florida’s great border-crossing sandwich: Cuban bread, pork, ham, Swiss, pickles, mustard, then pressed flat and hot. Tampa adds salami, Miami keeps it simple, and every town claims it does it best. These four are worth the drive. One word of caution, I can be a picky pain in the backside with these. A snob if you will. Bread 100 percetn matters. Beyond that 2 rules. No “Bar-b-Cubans” (made with smoked pulled pork) and no mayonnaise.

La Segunda Central Bakery, Tampa
The only must stop for me whenever I am in Tampa. Cuban bread has come out of this Ybor City bakery since 1915, and much of Tampa Bay builds its sandwiches on it. Genoa salami, mojo pork, ham, Swiss, pickles. Get it pressed. The classic. Simply outstanding every single time. Grab some pastries while you are there.
2512 N 15th St, Tampa, FL 33605

Black Bean Deli, Orlando
Same Cuban soul, newer look. A bright, youthful room near the heart of downtown Orlando with indoor and outdoor seating, Cuban coffee, beer and wine. Orlando Weekly readers named it best Cuban sandwich in 2023. I totally get it. And it’s only $12, which is a bargain these days.
1835 E Colonial Dr, Orlando, FL 32803

Paladar Cuban Eatery, St. Augustine Beach
Miami classics on Anastasia Island. Owned by two Cuban women, with a scratch bakery, cafecito, and the frita, a Miami favorite they say is sold nowhere else in North Florida. Excellent and only about 15 minutes form the heart of Old Town St Augustine. 5575 A1A S, Unit 112, St. Augustine Beach, FL 32080

Hola! Cuban Cafe, Fernandina Beach
Amelia Island’s first Cuban-influenced restaurant. Slow-roasted meats cooked in-house, pressed sandwiches, cafe con leche and a dog-friendly patio in the historic downtown. Breakfast and lunch. Chris and Marisol brought the flavors of Miami-Dade with them to their self proclaimed ‘favorite place in the world” and you can taste it with every bite. 31 S 5th St, Fernandina Beach, FL 32034
