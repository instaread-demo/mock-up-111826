    Woman arrested for allegedly killing husband in Jawzjan

    Raqiba Poya Aziz
    29 Sep 2026 · 1 min read · 105 views

    SHEBERGHAN (Pajhwok): A woman has been arrested on suspicion of killing her husband in northern Jawzjan province, a police official said on Tuesday.

    Qari Abdul Sattar Halimi, spokesman for the Jawzjan police headquarters, told Pajhwok Afghan News that intelligence and security forces in Khanqah district identified and arrested the woman in connection with her husband’s killing in Shah Mirza village.

    Halimi said the woman allegedly shot and killed her husband with a pistol around midnight on Monday.
