Finding Security, Satisfaction, and Purpose through God’s Word

An introduction to Spiritual Stuffing.

Deuteronomy 8:3 NLT

He gave you manna to eat in the wilderness, something your ancestors had never known, to humble and test you so that in the end it might go well with you. Recently, I found myself lying flat on my back with new nerve pain that made it difficult to walk or sit. I had gone to the doctor, and even she didn’t have a solution. “It just happens sometimes,” she said sympathetically. “We don’t really know why.” 

Suddenly I couldn’t rely on myself as much as I used to. I had to not only let my friends help me physically but also let them into the moments of debilitating anxiety that I experience when I’m in unfamiliar situations. As someone who feels that my worth is tied to how much I can accomplish, I felt incredibly insecure having something I could not seem to conquer.  

At some point, I thought to myself—I think this is what the Bible calls “humbling.”

Feeling very weak, I realized that I really needed the Scriptures to steady my emotions. I started studying verses about the enduring love of God, and realized that this experience could teach me something new about him—how he loves me on my good days and bad, how his love is not dependent on my performance. Maybe I could only learn it when life made me aware of my need. 

God allows us to go through things that make us aware of our limitations and our need for him—situations we can’t solve or fix on our own no matter how hard we try. It might be a physical problem, like mine, or it might be a nagging feeling of emptiness or an insecurity we can’t quiet. 

He doesn’t do it to be mean. He actually does it so that our lives will go better (Deuteronomy 8:16) because we will learn to rely on him and his Word in a way we otherwise wouldn’t have. 

Find security, satisfaction, and purpose through God’s word

So, in honor of the power of the Scriptures and one of the biggest food-related holidays of the year in the US, we have a new daily reading plan for you called “Spiritual ‘Stuffing’: Find Security, Satisfaction, and Purpose through God’s Word.” 

November is not only the month we celebrate Thanksgiving (with some delicious stuffing on the table) but it also happens to be Global Bible Month—a month designed to encourage people to engage with Scripture every day and experience its life-changing benefits.

You satisfy me more than the richest feast. I will praise you with songs of joy.

Psalm 63:5 NLT

This plan will teach us how to find three things in Scripture that our souls need:

  1. Security: The Bible is a firm foundation that brings us security, even when the storms of life hit. 
  2. Satisfaction: The Bible is not a list of rules. It fills our soul’s deepest desires for love, so we don’t have to chase it from other people or things. 
  3. Purpose: Through the Scriptures and the life of Jesus, God shows us how to live a truly significant life by serving other people. True greatness isn’t self-focused or self-consumed; it’s full of love.
