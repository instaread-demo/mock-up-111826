Paid Sick Leave Notice Failures: Seattle Hotel, Staffing Agencies Pay $133K

By

Read it on Beacon

Listen to audio version of this article

Instaread

A Seattle hotel operator and two staffing agencies will pay $133,026 to 11 workers to resolve alleged violations of the city’s paid sick leave ordinance and two other local laws protecting hotel employees.

Investigators found evidence the companies:

  • Failed to regularly tell one group of workers how much paid sick time they’d earned, used and had left
  • Shorted premium pay to housekeepers who went over the daily square-footage limit, and
  • Failed to make required medical payments for temp workers.

Payroll teams should check that pay stubs or other paid sick leave notices show leave earned, used and remaining, including for temp workers placed by staffing agencies.

More info: OLS news release
