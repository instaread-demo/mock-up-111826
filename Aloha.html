With an overwhelming amount of options to choose from, picking the best Optometry electronic health record or electronic medical record for your practice can be a daunting task.

While it may be daunting, it’s not something that you should take lightly. Having a reliable practice management software is so important because you cannot run a successful, sustainable Optometry practice without one.

A powerful practice management software helps you improve patient care by giving you instant access to vital patient information which streamlines many of your daily operations.

A practice management system is proven to improve patient care by showing eye care professionals key information and patient records, on-demand and all in one place.

In today’s fast-paced, technology-first world, healthcare practices need innovative solutions that will transform their optometry clinics into a revolutionary, state-of-the-art, revenue-generating machine to reach more patients and change more lives!

Imagine for a minute that you’re in your dream practice. What does that look like to you?

We imagine your dream practice doesn’t involve using multiple clunky, old systems, frantic scheduling changes, constant communication nightmares…the list goes on.

The best optometry EHR should make your life easier. As an Optometrist or team member, you wear a lot of different hats.

You need a complete practice management solution that can provide you with an on-demand patient portal built for flexibility and scalability.

There are 3 major factors to consider when choosing the right practice management solution for your optometry practices.

Cost: What is the true cost (i.e. Setup fees, IT fees, etc.) of the EHR software?

Data Storage: Is the EHR software Cloud-based or Server-based?

Connections: Does the EHR software integrate with Review Wave?

Let’s start with everyone’s favorite question…

What will it cost me?

When it comes to choosing a practice management solution, you should aim to obtain the true cost of purchasing that optometry software. Not just the starting price.

We recommend asking these 6 questions before purchasing any optometry software for the success of your business, but especially for electronic health records:

  1. Do I have to pay a monthly fee?
  2. Is there an annual contract?
  3. Is there a large lump payment up front?
  4. Do I have to pay for additional users?
  5. Do I have to pay for technical support?
  6. Are there any hidden costs (e.g. dedicated hardware/server)?

Asking these 6 questions will help you understand the entire cost of the software and ensure you know exactly what you’re getting into; from start to finish.

We understand that often the best optometry software comes at a price. But consider this…

Take your healthcare providers hat off for a second and picture yourself planning that MUCH NEEDED vacation.

When you’re booking a flight, are you willing to pay a little extra to avoid traveling at an inconvenient time, or having multiple layovers tripling your overall travel time?

How much do you value your time?

That’s an important question you need to ask yourself. And that’s an important question the majority of your patients consider when searching for an optometrist near them.

The best optometry software for your practice will be an investment into your business. It should set you up for success for years to come. A practice management system should not be a quick fix to your problems.

Why does data storage matter?

An optometry software that offers a cloud-based solution is the future of practice management. To make patient management easier than ever, you need a cloud-based software to keep up with the on-demand needs of today’s average patient.

What’s the difference between a cloud-based electronic health record and a server-based one?

Server-based software refers to the applications running on a physical, centralized server. A server refers to a dedicated computer tasked with managing network resources.

A server-based software is the traditional way to run applications, but with the increased prevalence of technology in our day-to-day lives, server-based software is extremely limiting.

Server-based software doesn’t allow you to offer an on-the-go experience that most people need and crave. As a business owner, you need the flexibility to manage operations anytime, anywhere; without being tied down to a desk or computer.

A Cloud-based EHR is in fact the internet and it’s everywhere! The cloud refers to giving users access to patient management tools via the internet.

Using a cloud-based EHR means that any user can access the data remotely without being physically present at the office. This makes data processing and management more convenient and efficient than ever.

With a cloud-based EHR, your business is not limited to business hours. You can attract new patients and communicate with current patients at any point in time, from anywhere you have internet. This sets you above your competition and offers a new level of convenience for your patients; Ultimately enhancing your patient experience and increasing your bottom line.

Most practices still use the outdated server-based electronic health records despite every single business trend telling them to switch to a cloud based EHR.

This question comes in handy AGAIN…How much do you value your time?

Technology makes optometry practice management easier than ever. With a top of the line practice management software, patient registration, patient prescription history, medical history, insurance benefits, user interface, and all those other daunting tasks quickly become a breeze.

Those dreaded tasks you have to keep doing because you’re glued to your old systems are holding you back from reaching your full potential.

They’re holding you back from helping MORE patients. A cloud-based EHR is a simple solution to set you up for ultimate success.

  5. NextGen Healthcare

    6. NextGen is a great option for small practices. It offers all the major features you’d want in your practice management system to manage patients, plus fast implementation and great customer support.

  7. RevolutionEHR

    8. RevolutionEHR has done an excellent job creating a simple, easy-to-use software that gives its clients the freedom to focus on patient care.

    You didn’t become an optometrist to spend your day fighting with computer systems. RevolutionEHR takes away the daily hassles that come with managing patient records.

    The monthly fee is based on the number of doctors in your practice and provides you with unlimited access to RevolutionEHR for all your doctors and staff across all locations and all devices.

    9. myCare iMedicWare has developed an intensive health record EHR solution built exclusively for eye care professionals. Their system elegantly addresses the needs of running an optometry practice or multiple locations and optimizes clinical, financial, and operational alignment.

    The software layout enables doctors quick and easy navigation with in-depth customization, to chart quickly and efficiently, and reduce human error.

    They offer a wide variety of specialty features like glaucoma, retinal, and refractive flow sheets, editable charts post-patient visit, the ability to view multiple test images, and more. Making this a key addition to your practice. You can see that they have optometrists’ unique needs at the forefront of what they do.

    Because their software is very niche and offers specialty features, their pricing is a little more than other EHR systems. The pricing starts at $900 per month per user and varies according to the number of providers, training, and implementation.

    They also don’t integrate with a fully-equipped patient experience platform like Review Wave, making it harder to interact with your patients and market your practice. But because they offer more specifically for optometrists, they can claim their spot as a top optometry EHR and practice management software.

  9. LiquidEHR

    10. LiquidEHR is a cutting-edge health record EHR solution built for speed, ease of use, and scalability.

    They provide doctors with features including a mailing list generator, audit trails, workflow systems, document management, compliance checks, e-prescribing, configurable eye exam records, historical IOP charts, eye charts, image management, and more!

    According to their reviews, some people struggle with the patient diagnostics feature, but overall there are many more pros listed.

    LiquidEHR has very straightforward pricing. It’s $199 per month for a server (in-office) version and $299 per month for the cloud version. Both options do not require a set-up fee and include unlimited workstations and unlimited doctors.

    The biggest downside is that it doesn’t integrate with a dynamic marketing automation and patient engagement tool like Review Wave. Creating more work on the backend to grow your practice and improve patient retention.

    11. iTRUST was built to save time and money. From generating itemized receipts to patient billing to daily office batch report summaries, iTRUST offers an extensive list of features to maximize your daily operations.
